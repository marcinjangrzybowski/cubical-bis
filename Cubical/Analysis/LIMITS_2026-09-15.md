# Boundedness, derivative limits, and series — 2026-09-15

This continues [Integration and FTC consequences](INTEGRATION_2026-09-15.md)
over `Cubical.Analysis.Real.Base.ℝ`. The results are exported by
`Cubical.Analysis.Real.Integration` and the main Analysis facade.

## Uniform continuity gives boundedness

[`UniformContinuity.Bounded`](Real/Integration/UniformContinuity/Bounded.agda)
proves `radiuswiseUniformContinuityIsBoundedOn`: for `a ≤ b`, radiuswise
uniform continuity of `f` on `[a,b]` gives a truncated positive rational
bound `B` with `|f(x)| ≤ B` throughout the interval.
`uniformContinuityIsBoundedOn` specializes it to standard uniform continuity.

The proof uses continuity at tolerance one, one sufficiently fine dyadic
partition, and a finite maximum of the absolute values at its nodes.
[`Partition.Cover`](Real/Integration/Partition/Cover.agda) proves a closed
inequality cell by cell without deciding which cell contains a real point.
It includes zero-cell partitions and repeated endpoints. No sequence of
choices or global continuity modulus is extracted.

Two applications now obtain bounds automatically:

- [`UniformlyContinuousIntervalIntegral`](Real/Integration/IntervalIntegral.agda)
  exposes `valueIsLipschitz` and `valueUniformlyContinuous` for its integral
  function, with no supplied bound.
- [`ContinuousSubstitution`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/ContinuousSubstitution.agda)
  constructs the integral of `f(G(x)) g(x)` when `G` has uniform derivative
  `g`, `f` is standard uniformly continuous on the target interval, and `g`
  is radiuswise uniformly continuous on the source interval. It derives both
  bounds used by substitution. The endpoint formula still allows unordered
  image endpoints and requires no monotonicity or injectivity of `G`.

## Stability of integral increments and derivatives

[`Stability.Estimates`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/Stability/Estimates.agda)
proves the distance-sensitive estimate

```text
F(y)-F(x) = integral[x,y] f,   G(y)-G(x) = integral[x,y] g,
|f-g| ≤ E on [a,b]
⇒ |(F(y)-F(x)) - (G(y)-G(x))| ≤ E |y-x|.
```

The conclusion holds for any two points of the ambient interval, in either
order. A [scalar remainder estimate](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/Stability/Remainder.agda)
then transfers a derivative estimate from `G` to `F` using this bound and
a bound on their derivative values.

[`Stability`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/Stability.agda) applies those
estimates to a uniformly convergent family of derivatives. If `F` already
has integral increments of `f`, and uniform derivatives `f_n` of functions
`F_n` converge uniformly to `f`, then `F` has uniform derivative `f`.
There is no separate continuity assumption on `f`. All convergence tails
and derivative radii in this theorem remain radiuswise truncated.

## Passing to limits

[`Limits`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/Limits.agda) adds:

- `indefiniteIntegralsConvergeUniformly`: uniform convergence of integrands
  gives uniform convergence of their supplied indefinite integrals. One
  tail works simultaneously for every upper endpoint in `[a,b]`.
- `accumulationsConvergeUniformly`: the specialization to constructed
  accumulation functions.
- `uniformLimitIsIntegrableOnSubintervals`: integrability on every ordered
  subinterval passes to a uniform limit with a supplied native convergence
  modulus.
- `pointwiseLimitPreservesIntegralIncrements`: pointwise convergence of
  functions and uniform convergence of integrands preserve integral
  increments. Endpoint convergence supplies each integral-value limit.
- `pointwiseLimitPreservesUniformDerivative`: if each `F_n` has uniform
  derivative `f_n`, `F_n → F` pointwise on the interval, and `f_n → f`
  uniformly, then `F` has uniform derivative `f`.
- `uniformLimitsPreserveUniformDerivative`: a convenient specialization
  when convergence of `F_n` is uniform too.

The preservation results use radiuswise convergence. A native modulus is
needed only in the stated existence theorem that constructs new integrals.

## Constructing a limit from one endpoint

The module
[`UniformDerivativeLimit`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/DerivativeLimit.agda)
takes uniform derivatives `F_n' = f_n` on an ordered interval, a
`UniformLimitWitness` for `f_n → f`, and ordinary convergence `F_n(a) → c`.
It constructs

```text
F(x) = c + integral[a,x] f       for x in [a,b].
```

It proves uniform convergence `F_n → F`, the uniform derivative law `F' = f`,
the initial value `F(a) = c`, and uniqueness among uniform antiderivatives
with that initial value. It also exposes the relative pointwise derivative,
including at the endpoints. The ambient real function is defined using the
clamped accumulation function; all analytic conclusions concern `[a,b]`.

The derivative convergence supplies the modulus used to construct the
integrals. Convergence of the initial values needs no chosen modulus.

## Differentiating series

[`FundamentalTheorem.Series`](Real/Integration/FundamentalTheorem/Series.agda)
proves uniform differentiation of finite partial sums and then two forms of
termwise differentiation:

- `termwiseDerivativeOfPointwiseSum`: if the original series has a supplied
  pointwise sum and the derivative series has a supplied uniform sum, the
  sum has the expected uniform derivative. No native modulus is required.
  `termwiseDerivativeOfUniformSum` is the uniform-sum specialization.
- `UniformDerivativeSeries`: a native uniform Cauchy witness for the
  derivative series, its identified sum `f`, and convergence of the original
  series at `a` construct a uniform sum on `[a,b]` with derivative `f`.

Each summand is assumed to have a radiuswise uniform derivative on the
interval. Supporting constant and addition rules for uniform derivatives
were added to the existing calculus modules, reusing the addition remainder
identity.

## Scope and verification

These results retain the uniform remainder hypothesis on the approximating
derivatives; they do not establish the corresponding general theorem from
ordinary pointwise differentiability alone. Integrability from only
radiuswise uniform continuity remains open. Boundedness from that weaker
continuity hypothesis is now proved.

No postulates, unsafe options, choice principles, or imports of another real
implementation were introduced. All affected modules and both top-level
facades are checked with `agda-codex`. The final results are recorded in
[PROGRESS](PROGRESS.md).

For predictable elaboration, function families have explicit types
`ℕ → ℝ → ℝ`, and interval arguments have explicit `ClosedInterval a b`
types. Endpoint estimates and scalar remainder estimates are checked in
separate modules. Smaller dependency checks also avoid rebuilding the
substitution stack in one process under the compiler's memory guard. For
the largest aggregate checks, `+RTS -c -RTS` enables GHC's compacting garbage
collector; the wrapper's heap cap and system-memory reserve remain unchanged.
