Broker2 progress and validation

8 September 2026 — one engine evolved for a quant developer presentation

Implemented seven successive versions in 857 Agda source lines. The reading guide and 28-minute presentation follow one recurring quoteCall / receive / receive-correct contract. Every version after V01 imports its predecessor and has an explicit projection, specialization, or transition-refinement theorem. No concrete scenario or calibrated portfolio was added.

Implementation milestones

  • V01: a self-contained account state, positive-part call calculation, actual receipt transition, leastness, receipt composition, and equivalent policy propositions identified by univalence.
  • V02: the same operations lifted to separate client netting sets; per-client projection commutes with quoting and receiving. Whole-book coverage eliminates modeled residual and severity-weighted loss.
  • V03: finite nonempty stress profiles with arbitrary Cauchy-real values; uniform least calls, receipt/report update identity, scenario projection, and an exact singleton regression to V02.
  • V04: rational upper reports on current incremental calls, a decidable receipt gate, report updates after partial payments, and optional complete economic decisions under exactness. A generic rational input factory computes exact initial reports without an external margin oracle.
  • V05: the same engine with minimum transfer amounts. Actual adequate receipts give client and aggregate loss bounds; zero MTA recovers V04's quote.
  • V06: a market and immutable identified-receipt ledger. A state path proves that receiving a batch equals the previous engine receiving only newly credited cash. process returns the next state, its transition equality, a certified next report, and the assessment. Replay identifies the state plus its safety certificate.
  • V07: a supplied bounded market update preserves the ledger and refreshes the incremental report with a conservative rational reserve. The same receipt engine then runs under the new market. An unchanged-market/zero-reserve specialization recovers V06. Final guarantees quantify over every supplied stress, client, and modeled severity.

The earlier Broker/ progression remains independent. Reusable finite-margin, shortfall, journal, revaluation and transfer lemmas are reused rather than copied into each version. Broker2.agda exposes version namespaces; Theory.Broker2 provides the reusable entry point.

Parallel development and review

One agent implemented and checked V04 and its generic rational report factory. A second agent wrote the presentation guide. A third independently reviewed the version sequence and its financial/refinement boundaries while the root built the other versions and their composition.

Reviews found no remaining correctness defect. They sharpened the following points, which are recorded in the guide and source:

  • Reports bound incremental calls, already accounting for existing cash. Receipt updates subtract new cash only; revaluation adds its reserve without subtracting the old ledger balance again.
  • Uniform stress quotes dominate individual snapshot quotes. Equality with V02 is the singleton specialization, not a general scenario projection claim.
  • A conservative quoted plan can be unfulfilled while coverage already holds. Economic completeness is asserted only with an exact report.
  • V06's projection represents catalog-derived rational cash. It is not a representation equivalence with every V05 arbitrary real balance.
  • Actual partial receipts remain recorded on an unmet-quote outcome, and the report advances to the resulting state.
  • Replay identifies state plus Guarantee. It does not identify arbitrary conservative reports or assert arbitrary revaluation-workflow idempotence.

REFERENCES.md connects the progression to BCBS FX guidance, robust feasibility, minimum transfers, event identity, duplicate suppression and margin/gap risk. The speaker guide explains the actual theorem statements and keeps numerical calibration and operational claims outside their scope.

8 September 2026 — relational implementation of the final model

Added Pipeline, using typed rows, named field access, selection, key membership, and grouped rational sums from the relation library. The final V07 engine remains the abstract specification. The data implementation is separate from its accounting, safety and refinement proofs.

Fulfilled calls transfer the same financial bounds to independently specified sums over source keys. Receipt replay/reordering preserve the concrete certified state at a fixed valuation.
