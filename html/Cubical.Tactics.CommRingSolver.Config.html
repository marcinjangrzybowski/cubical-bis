 CommonDenom : ⟨ R ⟩ → ⟨ R ⟩ → Type ℓ
 CommonDenom a b =
             Σ[ (a' , b' , c ) ∈ ⟨ R ⟩ × ⟨ R ⟩ × ⟨ R ⟩ ]
                (a ≡ a' · c) × (b ≡ b' · c)
